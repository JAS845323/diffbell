Why Another Webpage Change Tracker?

Since Chrome removed WebSQL and older local monitors stopped working, most options became either expensive cloud SaaS tools with tight check limits or overly complex dashboards.

1

As simple as it used to be

Open any page, click the element you care about, and set an interval. No XPath tutorials, no multi-screen onboarding, and no cluttered dashboard to learn.

2

Unlimited local checks, fewer false alarms

Checks run natively inside Chrome Manifest V3 on your own schedule. Target specific DOM regions and trim whitespace or dynamic noise so layout tweaks don't spam you.

3

No account — data stays on your computer

Zero sign-ups required to monitor pages. Your URLs, selectors, and page snapshots live exclusively in local browser storage and never touch third-party scraping servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this related to Page Monitor?

DiffBell is an independent, unofficial product built from scratch for Chrome Manifest V3. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Page Monitor, Visualping, or Distill. We built it for former Page Monitor users looking for a simple, local-first monitor after Chrome retired WebSQL.

Where is my monitoring data stored?

All monitored URLs, selected page regions, check intervals, and change snapshots are stored 100% locally in your browser (using Chrome's local extension storage and IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded to our servers. When you enable optional Pro phone notifications, your browser sends the alert directly to the Telegram, Discord, or ntfy webhook you configure — with zero middleman backend.

When will it launch?

We are currently validating early demand and prioritizing Pro features based on waitlist feedback. Waitlist subscribers will be invited to the first Chrome Web Store beta release and receive the $12 early-bird one-time link before public launch.

Does Chrome need to stay open for checks to run?

Yes. Because checks run locally inside Chrome (using Manifest V3 alarms) without a cloud server scraping sites on your behalf, your browser needs to be open on your computer. That local architecture is what lets us offer unlimited monitors for free with complete privacy.

Is Pro a monthly subscription?

No. Pro is a $19 one-time purchase ($12 early bird for waitlist sign-ups), activated with a Polar.sh license key. No recurring monthly charges.
