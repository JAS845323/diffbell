Privacy Policy for DiffBell

Last updated: 2026-11-26

1. Waitlist Email Collection

When you join the pre-launch waitlist on this website, your email address (and optional single-question feature preference) is submitted through Tally (tally.so). We use your email address solely to notify you when DiffBell launches and to share your early-bird launch link.

2. No Third-Party Sharing or Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your email address with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketers. This static landing page does not run third-party advertising pixels or behavioral analytics scripts.

3. Local-First Extension Architecture (Planned Beta)

The DiffBell browser extension is designed to run 100% locally inside your browser on Chrome Manifest V3:

  • Monitored URLs, selected page regions, check intervals, and diff snapshots stay in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage / IndexedDB) and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • If you choose to configure optional Pro phone notifications (Telegram, Discord, or ntfy), alert messages are sent directly from your browser to the webhook URL you specify.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Waitlist email addresses are kept only until launch notification is sent (or until you request removal). You can unsubscribe or request immediate deletion at any time by replying to the launch email or contacting the publisher email listed once the Chrome Web Store listing goes live.
